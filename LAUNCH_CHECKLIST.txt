SQUAROID FINAL WEBSITE — LAUNCH CHECKLIST

Included in this package
- Seven informational HTML pages: Home, About, Awards 2026, Sponsors, Contact, Privacy, Terms
- Responsive teal/off-white styling, original founder photos, navigation consistency pass
- Mobile navigation JavaScript, reduced-motion handling, footer/internal links
- Page titles/descriptions and social preview metadata

Before publishing
1. Confirm the final production domain and HTTPS.
2. Add canonical URLs and Open Graph URL values using that exact domain on every page.
3. Replace sitemap.xml with absolute URLs on the confirmed domain, then add the Sitemap directive to robots.txt.
4. Update Organization structured data with the confirmed URL and official social profile URLs. Do not publish unverified social URLs.
5. Verify the official Instagram handle/link.
6. Confirm competition dates, eligibility wording, upload requirements, contact email and all rules before public promotion.
7. Replace draft Privacy and Terms with organiser-reviewed legal text before collecting personal information or accepting entries.
8. The website is informational only. Registration, payments, uploads, accounts, saved forms and jury tools are not implemented in this package.
9. Test every page and menu on desktop, tablet and mobile after upload. Verify images, phone/WhatsApp links and outbound social links.
10. Configure Google Search Console and analytics only after the production domain is live and the privacy notice reflects the actual tools used.

Domain note
The final domain was not supplied during this build. Invalid YOUR-DOMAIN-HERE canonical, sitemap and robots URLs have therefore been removed/neutralized rather than published as broken URLs. Complete steps 1–4 before requesting indexing.
