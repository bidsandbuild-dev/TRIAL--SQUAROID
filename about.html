ABOUT SQUAROID · OUR STORY

A community for the makers of tomorrow.

Squaroid brings architecture, design, education and community together to give emerging creative voices a place to share ideas, develop their work and connect with the wider built-environment ecosystem.

01 / Our purpose

Good design should create a better tomorrow.

We believe architecture is more than the making of buildings. It shapes how people learn, live, work, gather and relate to the environment around them. Squaroid aims to support the next generation of designers by creating meaningful opportunities for visibility, learning and exchange.

Our approach combines student-focused initiatives, design competitions, professional connections and accessible knowledge. We want to help promising work reach wider audiences while encouraging thoughtful responses to people, planet and place.

02 / What we're building

More than a competition.

01 / RECOGNITION

Celebrate emerging talent

Give students and recent graduates a platform to present their work, receive recognition and build confidence in their design journey.

02 / LEARNING

Encourage critical thinking

Bring attention to design intent, context, architectural resolution, accessibility, environmental responsibility and clear communication.

03 / CONNECTION

Connect people and practice

Create opportunities for students, educators, studios, institutions, industry partners and the wider architecture community to engage.

03 / Our flagship initiative

The Design ArCHIVE — Student Architecture Awards 2026

Our 2026 student awards are framed around “Architecture for a Better Tomorrow — People · Planet · Places · Possibilities.” The programme is designed to recognise individual student and recent-graduate projects across five categories: Institutional; Housing and Residential; Commercial and Mixed Use; Cultural and Social; and Urban Design and Public Realm.

The proposed evaluation process includes initial shortlisting, online jury review and final presentations. Official dates, jury details, registration links and final submission instructions will be announced once confirmed.
