Why partner with Squaroid?

Invest in ideas that shape the built environment.

The Design ArCHIVE offers an opportunity to support student architecture and connect your organisation with a community interested in design, materials, technology, sustainability and the future of the built environment.

We welcome conversations with building-material brands, architecture and design technology companies, studios, publishers, educational institutions and organisations aligned with responsible design.
