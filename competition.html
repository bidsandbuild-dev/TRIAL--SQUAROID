The brief

Design for a better tomorrow.

The Design ArCHIVE recognises architectural work with clear design intent, contextual understanding, spatial quality and responsibility toward people and the environment.

Projects can explore social and cultural context, environmental performance, new ways of living and working, public life, adaptive reuse and urban relationships.

Who can enter?

Architecture students and recent architecture graduates may submit an individual academic studio, thesis or other eligible architecture project, provided they have the right to submit and publish it. A recommended 24-month recent-graduate window is still awaiting organiser confirmation. Acknowledge any collaboration and source material.

Frequently asked questions

Before you prepare your entry.

Dates, final eligibility, registration and submission instructions are not confirmed. The official brief will settle those details.

Can I submit as a team?

No. Entries must be submitted by an individual participant; group and team entries are not eligible.

Which projects and categories can I enter?

Academic studio projects, thesis projects and other eligible architectural projects may be submitted when you have the right to submit and publish the work. Choose one of the five categories and acknowledge collaboration or source material.

Will entries be reviewed anonymously?

Yes. Use your assigned entry code and remove your name, college identifiers, logos, signatures, watermarks and other identifying details from the sheets. Instructions for receiving the code will be provided before submissions open.

When does registration open, and where do I submit?

Dates, the official registration link and submission instructions are still to be announced. This website does not accept entries or payments. Wait for Squaroid’s official announcement before paying or sending files.

May I submit a project created with others?

Only individual entries are accepted. Your submitted work must be your own; acknowledge any collaboration and source material, and make sure you have permission to submit and publish it.

When are the deadlines and presentations?

Registration, submission, shortlist, final presentation and awards dates are not confirmed yet. Final presentation format and duration will be shared in the published brief.
